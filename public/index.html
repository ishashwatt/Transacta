So, how does Transacta work?

01. Discovery

Parallel Market Scouting.

Tell your AI what you want to buy, and it checks multiple trusted stores at the same time to find real in-stock items, compare prices, and check seller reviews.

  • No Manual Searching: Compares hundreds of products, tech specs, and store prices in just a few seconds.
  • Trusted Sellers Only: Connects only with verified merchants who have high ratings and fast delivery records.
  • Fair Comparisons: Automatically ranks every store offer by total price, warranty coverage, and shipping speed.
02. Negotiation

Value Beyond Discounts.

If a store cannot lower its price any further, your agent negotiates extra perks—like free accessories, faster shipping, or extended warranties.

  • Instant Back-and-Forth: Your agent talks directly with the store's system to get you the lowest possible price.
  • Free Bonus Items: Gets extra value like premium cables, carrying cases, or extra protection plans included for free.
  • Win-Win Deals: Stores protect their margins while you walk away with a much better overall deal.
03. Settlement

Absolute Protection, Instant Settlement.

You set your maximum spending limit before starting. If sellers cannot meet your terms, your agent safely walks away—you never overpay.

  • Strict Spending Limit: Your money stays safe. The agent cannot spend a single rupee over your set budget.
  • Clear Decision Logs: See exactly why a deal was accepted or why the agent walked away in plain words.
  • Secure Razorpay Checkout: Once a deal is finalized, payment is processed instantly and safely through Razorpay escrow.

Frequently asked questions

Normal chatbots only search products and send you links. Transacta has an AI buyer agent that actually talks to multiple sellers on your behalf, negotiates for the lowest price and free bundled accessories, and lets you pay safely through Razorpay.
Sellers cannot reduce their cash price below their wholesale cost without taking a loss. When a seller hits their lowest cash price, Transacta asks them to add high-margin extras instead—such as an HDMI cable, mousepad, or extended warranty—giving you more total value for your budget.
Transacta has built-in walk-away protection. If all seller offers are higher than your maximum budget limit, the agent stops negotiating immediately. You are never charged any money, and you can see the exact price gap in the audit ledger.
Once you review and accept the final negotiated deal, Transacta creates an official order with Razorpay. You pay directly through the standard Razorpay checkout screen using UPI, credit cards, or net banking, and your receipt is verified instantly on our server.
You set your exact spending limit before the search starts. The AI agent is strictly locked to this number and cannot agree to any deal above it. You also always review and confirm the final price before any payment is made.
